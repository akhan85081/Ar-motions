1. Client Eligibility & Direct Partnership Policy

Direct Clients Only

AR Motions works exclusively through direct partnerships to ensure the highest creative standards, seamless communication, and efficient execution.

  • Zero Middleman Policy: I do not provide editing services to brokers, third-party middlemen, or outsourcing intermediaries.
  • Eligible Clients: I work solely and directly with verified Brands, Content Creators, and Business Owners.
  • All creative briefs, source assets, and approvals must originate directly from the brand owner or authorized internal team member.

3. Payment Terms & Asset Protection

Milestone Based
  • 50% Advance Payment: Required prior to project initiation, asset download, or timeline scheduling.
  • 50% Final Delivery Payment: Due upon final preview review and prior to releasing clean, unwatermarked master assets.

Digital Watermark Policy:

All review cuts, drafts, and approval previews will feature a permanent AR Motions watermark. Master clean renders (high-bitrate export, project archives, or native project files) will be released strictly after 100% full invoice clearance.
