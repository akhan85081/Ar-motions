About

One editor. Every frame.

Arshad Khan is the founder of AR MOTIONS — a founder-led video production and editing studio based in Ratlam, India, working with clients across the globe.

Every project is cut, graded and finished by Arshad himself: from premium UI and SaaS animations to long-form documentary edits and motion graphics. No hand-offs — just a direct line between your vision and the final cut.

Support comes standard: revisions, questions and tweaks are handled personally, whenever you need them.
