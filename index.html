FAQ

Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before we start.

What is your turnaround time?+

Most short-form edits are delivered within 48–72 hours. Documentaries and long-form projects typically take 5–10 days, depending on footage length and complexity. On a tight deadline? Rush delivery is available — just mention it when you reach out.

How many revisions do I get?+

Every project includes structured revision rounds, so we can fine-tune pacing, music, graphics, and color until it feels right. I keep refining within the agreed scope until you are genuinely happy with the final cut.

What do you need from me to start?+

Just your raw footage (or even a rough idea), a few reference videos you like, and the goal of the video. I handle everything else — structure, pacing, sound design, color grading, captions, and final delivery in the exact format you need.

How do pricing and payment work?+

Pricing depends on video length, style, and complexity — every quote is custom, transparent, and has no hidden charges. A small advance confirms your slot, and the balance is due on final delivery. Message me on WhatsApp or email for a fast, free quote.
