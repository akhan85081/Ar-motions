08 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

01

What software do you use for video editing?

I use industry-standard software such as Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, and Final Cut Pro depending on the specific requirements of the project.

02

How long does it take to get a video edited?

Turnaround times vary based on the project’s complexity and length. Short videos can be delivered within 24-48 hours, while larger projects like documentaries might take a week or more. I offer fast turnarounds tailored to your needs.

03

Do you provide source files?

Yes, project source files can be provided upon request. This might involve an additional fee depending on the contract agreement.

04

How do revisions work?

I ensure you are 100% satisfied. Standard packages include 2-3 rounds of revisions, and my 24/7 support is always available to discuss any required tweaks.
